Questions

Before you ask.

Is this made with AI?

Yes, and we are upfront about it. We use AI image and video tools, then direct, check and correct every frame by hand. Your product itself always comes from your own photo, so what customers see is what they will receive.

Will it really look like my product?

That is the part we are strictest about. We keep your packaging, label and shape exactly as they are. If anything looks off in the preview, we fix it before you pay the balance.

What do you need from me?

One clear phone photo per product, taken in daylight if you can, plus a line about your brand and any deadline. That is it.

How fast is it, really?

You get a watermarked preview within 48 hours of sending your photo. Final files usually follow within a day of your approval.

Can I use the images in paid ads?

Yes. You get full commercial use of everything we deliver: organic posts, paid social, your website, email and print.

How do I pay?

50% to start and 50% on delivery, by UK bank transfer in GBP or by card. US clients can pay in USD.

What if I don't like it?

You see a free sample with your own product before committing, and every package includes rounds of changes. If the preview is not right for you, you walk away and pay nothing.
