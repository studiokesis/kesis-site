Questions

What every brand asks first.

Is this made with AI?

Yes, and we say so openly. We use AI image and video tools, then direct, check and correct every frame by hand. Your product always comes from your own photos and packaging.

Will it really look like my product?

That is the part we are strictest about. Packaging, label and shape stay exactly as they are. If anything looks off, we fix it before you pay the balance.

What do you need from me?

Photos of your product from a few angles, any brand material you have, and a short brief: who you sell to, the feeling you want and your deadline. We take it from there.

How fast is it?

You see a first draft a few days after we agree the creative direction. Most projects are delivered within one to two weeks, depending on the number of films and rounds. Working to a Christmas deadline? Tell us and we will plan the rounds around it.

Can I use everything in paid adverts?

Yes. Full commercial use: organic posts, paid social, your website, email and print.

What if the first draft isn't right?

The first draft is free. If the direction is not right for your brand, you walk away and pay nothing.
